Sunrise Alarm

The alarm you have to get up for. It keeps ringing until you finish a wake-up mission.

How it works

  • Pick a mission when you set an alarm: math, shaking, a memory grid, typing a phrase, walking, squats, scanning a code in another room, or a photo hunt.
  • When the alarm rings, the only way to silence it is to finish the mission. Snoozes are limited.
  • Track your streak and how fast you get out of bed.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Your alarms and history stay on your phone.
