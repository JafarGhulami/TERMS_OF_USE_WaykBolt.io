4. Missions and your safety

Some missions ask you to walk, do squats, shake your phone, or go to another room right after waking, when you may be drowsy and it may be dark.

  • Turn on a light, watch for stairs, pets, and obstacles, and move at a pace that is safe for you.
  • Hold your phone securely when shaking it, and keep clear of people and hard objects.
  • Don’t choose a physical mission if you have a medical condition, injury, pregnancy, or balance problem that makes it unsafe. Ask your doctor if you’re unsure. Stop right away if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.
  • The App is not a medical, fitness, or sleep-treatment product and doesn’t give medical advice. Step and squat counts are estimates.
  • You can switch to an easier backup mission if one isn’t working for you.

You choose which missions to use and are responsible for doing them safely. To the fullest extent the law allows, you take on the risks of doing physical activity with the App.
