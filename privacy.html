Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the WaykBolt alarm clock app (the “App”) handles information. The App is published by GITI Tech (“we,” “us,” or “our”). If you have a question at any point, email Jghulami2000@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or give us your name, email, or phone number to use the App.
  • Your data stays on your phone. Your alarms, settings, and wake-up history are stored only on your device. We don’t have a copy.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The App contains no advertising or analytics tools, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don’t sell or share your personal information.
  • One exception, only if you use it: when photo checking is turned on for the Photo Hunt mission, the photo you take for the mission is sent to our checking service and to Anthropic’s AI service to confirm it shows the right object. It is not stored by us.

1. Information the App uses

The App needs some information to work. Unless this section says otherwise, it is created on your device, stays on your device, and is never sent to us.

InformationWhat it is and why it’s neededWhere it goes
Alarms and settings Alarm times, repeat days, labels you type, chosen sounds, mission settings, snooze rules, theme, sleep goal and other preferences, so your alarms ring the way you set them. Stays on your device.
Wake-up history When each alarm rang, whether you completed or missed it, how long it took, which mission you did, and how many times you snoozed. Used to show your streak and progress. Stays on your device.
Registered barcode If you use the Scan a Code mission, the text content of the barcode or QR code you register (for example, a product number) and the name you give it, so the App can check you scanned the same code in the morning. Stays on your device.
Camera images Live camera frames used to read barcodes, and the photo you take for Photo Hunt. Processed on your device. Not saved to your photo library. A Photo Hunt photo is sent off the device only when photo checking is on (see section 3).
Motion and step data Accelerometer readings and step counts, used live to count shakes, squats, and steps during a mission. Processed on your device in real time. Only the fact that you finished the mission is recorded.

If you have device backups turned on (such as iCloud Backup or Google’s Android backup), your operating system may include the App’s on-device data in those backups. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies, not by us.

2. Device permissions

The App asks for permission before using these features. You can say no, and you can change your mind at any time in your device’s Settings. Saying no only turns off the feature that needs the permission.

  • Notifications: to ring your alarms when the App is closed or your phone is locked. Alarms are scheduled on your device; we don’t send push notifications from a server and don’t collect a push token.
  • Exact alarms (Android): to make sure alarms ring at the exact minute you set.
  • Camera: for the Scan a Code and Photo Hunt missions only.
  • Motion & Fitness / Physical activity: for the Walk, Squats, and Shake missions only.
  • Vibration and keeping the screen on: so the alarm can vibrate and stay visible while it rings.

The App does not use your microphone, location, contacts, calendar, or photo library.

3. Photo Hunt photo checking

Photo Hunt asks you to take a photo of something, such as a coffee mug or the sky, to turn off your alarm. The App can check that photo automatically. You can see whether this is on in Settings → Reliability → Photo checking. If you never use Photo Hunt, no photo ever leaves your device.

When photo checking is on and you take a Photo Hunt photo:

  1. The App shrinks the photo and sends it, together with the name of the object you were asked to find, over an encrypted connection to our photo checking service.
  2. Our service passes the photo to Anthropic, an AI company that acts as our service provider, which looks at the photo and answers whether it shows the requested object.
  3. The answer comes back to your device and the alarm stops if it matches.

We don’t save the photo, and we don’t attach your name, an account, or a device identifier to it. Our service keeps short technical logs (the object name, whether it matched, and how long the check took) that do not include the photo. Anthropic processes the photo under its commercial terms. It does not use it to train its AI models, and it keeps it only for a limited time under its own policies, for example to detect misuse. See Anthropic’s Privacy Policy.

Please point the camera at the object, not at people. If you photograph someone, make sure they’re okay with it. If photo checking is off, or the checker can’t be reached, the photo stays on your device and the App accepts it without checking.

4. What we don’t collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account details.
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Advertising identifiers, or information used to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports. The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools. (On Android, Google’s on-device barcode scanner may send Google limited technical data; see section 5.)
  • Sensitive personal information such as health records, financial information, or government ID numbers. Step counts and motion readings are used only on your device during a mission and are not stored.

5. Who receives information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising. Information leaves your device only in these cases:

  • Photo checking (only if you use Photo Hunt while it’s on): our checking service, the company that hosts it, and Anthropic receive the photo as described in section 3. Like any internet service, the hosting company may briefly record your device’s IP address in its network logs.
  • Barcode scanning on Android: the App reads barcodes using Google’s ML Kit, which runs on your device. Your camera images are not sent to Google, but ML Kit may send Google limited technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version, and performance data. See Google’s ML Kit terms.
  • When you choose to share: if you tap “Share my wake-up,” the App opens your phone’s share sheet with a short text message. You choose where it goes, and that app’s own privacy policy applies.
  • App stores: Apple and Google handle downloads, updates, and any purchases, and may give us anonymized, aggregated statistics (such as download counts) under their own policies.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, to respond to valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or ourselves. Because we hold almost no information about you, there is usually nothing to disclose.
  • Business transfers: if the App is sold or transferred, any information we hold would transfer under this policy or one that protects it at least as well.

6. How long information is kept

  • On your device: alarms, settings, and history stay until you delete them, reset the App, or uninstall it. History keeps your most recent 400 wake-ups.
  • Photo checking: we don’t keep the photo after the check is complete. Our short technical logs (without photos) are kept for no more than 30 days. Anthropic and our hosting provider keep data only for the limited periods described in their own policies.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete everything: open Settings → Reset all data in the App, or uninstall the App. Either one removes your alarms, settings, and history from your device.
  • Turn off a feature: deny or revoke the camera, motion, or notification permission in your device’s Settings.
  • Avoid photo uploads: don’t choose the Photo Hunt mission. Other missions never send anything off your device.

8. Security

Information on your device is protected by your device’s own security, such as your passcode and encryption. Photos sent for checking travel over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and our checking service only accepts requests from the App. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but keeping almost everything on your device keeps the risk small.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the App has no accounts and keeps data on the device, we don’t receive information that identifies a child. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
