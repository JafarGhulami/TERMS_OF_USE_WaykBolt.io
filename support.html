Quick check: open FeetFirst and go to Settings → Test alarm, then lock your phone. If you hear it ring in 10 seconds, your morning alarms will ring the same way.

My alarm didn’t ring (iPhone)

  1. Allow notifications: Settings → Notifications → FeetFirst → turn on Allow Notifications, Sounds, and Time Sensitive Notifications.
  2. Turn off Silent mode (the switch or Action button on the side of your phone). In Silent mode, iPhone mutes notification sounds, so the alarm may only vibrate unless FeetFirst is already open on screen.
  3. Focus and Sleep: if you use a Sleep or Do Not Disturb Focus, add FeetFirst under Allowed Apps.
  4. Volume: turn the volume up. The sound plays at your phone’s volume level.
  5. Open the app once a day. Each time you finish a mission, FeetFirst schedules your upcoming alarms. If you haven’t opened it in several days, open it once so they’re all set.

My alarm didn’t ring (Android)

  1. Allow notifications for FeetFirst in Settings → Apps → FeetFirst → Notifications, and keep the alarm channels set to sound.
  2. Alarms & reminders: if your phone has this setting, make sure FeetFirst is allowed to set alarms (Settings → Apps → Special app access → Alarms & reminders).
  3. Battery: set FeetFirst’s battery usage to Unrestricted (Settings → Apps → FeetFirst → Battery). Some phones, especially Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus and Huawei, delay or block alarms from apps that are “optimized.” Also remove FeetFirst from any “sleeping apps” list.
  4. Volume: notifications use your alarm volume. While FeetFirst is open on the alarm screen, it plays through your media volume, so keep both turned up.
  5. Do Not Disturb: allow alarms, or add FeetFirst as an exception.

I can’t finish my mission

  • Backup mission: after 3 minutes, or right away if your phone is missing a sensor the mission needs, a “Stuck? Switch to a backup mission” link appears. It gives you a few easy math problems.
  • Walk: step counts can take a few seconds to update. Keep walking with the phone in your hand or pocket. On Android, allow Physical activity; on iPhone, allow Motion & Fitness.
  • Squats: hold the phone flat against your chest and squat at a steady pace, pausing briefly at the top.
  • Shake: grip the phone firmly and shake it quickly. Gentle wiggles don’t count.
  • Scan a Code: you need to register the code first when you set up the alarm. Turn on the flashlight button in dim light and hold the code about 6 inches (15 cm) away.
  • Photo Hunt: turn on a light, fill the frame with the object, and avoid photographing a screen. Tap “Something else” to get a different object (twice per alarm).

Why can’t I snooze?

Snooze is limited on purpose so it can’t become a habit. You can turn it on and choose 1 to 3 snoozes of 3, 5 or 10 minutes when you edit an alarm.

How do I delete my data?

Everything FeetFirst stores is on your phone. To erase it, open Settings → Reset all data, or uninstall the app. There is no account to delete. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where do my Photo Hunt pictures go?

They aren’t saved to your photo library. If photo checking is on, the picture is sent securely to be checked and then discarded. Otherwise it never leaves your phone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
